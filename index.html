Yuqing Wang 汪雨晴

Master's student at Peking University

I am a master's student at Peking University, advised by Prof. Fan Zhang. Previously, I completed my undergraduate studies in the School of Electronic Information at Wuhan University, where I studied in the Artificial Intelligence Class.

My research interests are spatial intelligence, spatial representations, interpretable AI, and urban AI. I study how AI systems represent and reason about spatial information, with a current focus on visual navigation, functional landmarks, and cognitive maps. My work draws on multimodal large language models (MLLMs) and reinforcement learning (RL); I am also interested in vision-language models and language-guided navigation agents.

I welcome conversations and collaborations on spatial intelligence and spatial cognition.

Research

Navigational landmarkness and cognitive maps Ongoing

I am developing a research framework to understand when visual cues become useful landmarks for navigation, and how these landmarks can anchor interpretable cognitive maps. The planned work uses controlled interventions to connect landmark function with navigation behavior and spatial representations.

Urban visual navigation UrbanNav

Building on my undergraduate thesis, I investigate map-free navigation and spatial cognition in reinforcement-learning agents. I independently developed the Paris experiments, including a street-view navigation environment, CNN/RNN perception and memory models, and PPO training and evaluation.
